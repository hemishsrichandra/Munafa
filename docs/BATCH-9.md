## Completion status

**Readiness workspace and fail-closed safeguards implemented. All three P2 business capabilities remain disabled/blocked.** Their required foundations and relevant P1 data quality are not proven in this repository. This is not a completed procurement, lending or benchmarking engine. Batch 8 was not silently implemented to bypass that gate.

The current source has accepted purchase/physical-receipt records, exact append-only stock events, supplier records, authenticated memberships and central approvals. It has no verified current supplier-offer/availability registry, purchase-order lifecycle, complete cashflow/payable/receivable/commitment ledger, authorised lender process/consent registry, or approved anonymised cohort policy. Existing row counts, past invoices and synthetic payment observations cannot establish those prerequisites.

## Delivered / exact boundaries

- `backend/planning.py`: principal-store-scoped evidence/readiness service and typed inspection inputs; no new persistence layer, model, migration, external adapter, state library or dependency.
- Default-off `ENABLE_P2_PROCUREMENT`, `ENABLE_P2_FINANCIAL_PLANNING`, `ENABLE_P2_BENCHMARKING`. Only literal `1` requests activation; invalid configuration fails closed. Flags never supply missing prerequisites. Status is `disabled` when off and `blocked` when requested but unproven. There is no ready/enabled execution path in this batch.
- `GET /api/planning/readiness`: actual store-scoped record counts, up to five own SKU examples, source timestamps/units/versions, explicit risk/blocker/uncertainty metadata and peer suppression. Counts are explicitly not completeness/quality proof. No cross-store aggregate, price estimate, financial total or credit signal is returned.
- `POST /api/planning/assess`: readiness inspection only. Checks selected product/stock versions, unit and unconfirmed need; scopes supplier/attachment IDs; identifies expired/implausible **declared** offer dates. A current-looking declaration or stored attachment never verifies price, availability, permission, tax or shipping. No client-supplied price or `verified` field is accepted. It records a minimal existing audit event and an idempotent receipt, not a proposal/order.
- Owner/admin permission is required for financial inspection. Lender consent claims have a restricted purpose, remain unverified and never authorise submission. No lender checklist requirements, underwriting, eligibility score, terms or application are generated. Raw process-reference/consent text is omitted from the audit and response.
- Peer statistics, cohort size, methodology and date ranges remain absent. All unapproved cohorts are suppressed, including small cohorts and large sizes declared by a client. No other store's records are queried. The safe alternative links to the merchant's existing SKU movement timeline; it does not invent a within-store growth comparison.
- `POST /api/planning/{capability}/execute` rejects execution even for an owner with a flag or claimed approval. Central action types remain allow-listed and do not accept ordering/lending/peer commands. There is no messaging, ordering, terms acceptance, fund commitment or lender-data submission implementation to invoke.
- React `Planning.jsx`: phone-first readiness/evidence screen, accessible from More and the desktop sidebar, meaningful inspection controls, loading/empty/error/offline/stale/permission states and working own-history links. No fake charts, generated P2 results, dead order buttons or mock results in a live view. It uses the existing app shell/styles; no new offline queue/cache is introduced.
