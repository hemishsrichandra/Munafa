## Impact: supported versus unproven

The working Munafa loop can organise discrepancy evidence, make a claim traceable and distinguish supplier promises from documented recovery. Inventory separates invoicing from physical receipt, so accepting a bill cannot invent stock. Tests demonstrate those rules on synthetic scenarios; they do not prove reduced losses or faster work in real shops.

No consented merchant pilot or comparison group is recorded. Do not claim revenue uplift, guaranteed recovery, fraud prevention, forecasting accuracy, complete business profitability, verified bank receipt or regulatory readiness. Required measurements: task time/corrections, documented recovery amount/time, repeat use, missed/duplicate alerts, stock-count discrepancy, abandonment and support burden. Measure against the merchant's current notebook/WhatsApp workflow over a stated period.

## Implementation sequence and acceptance criteria

1. **Customer and source ledgers first.** Confirm manual reviewed entry versus an actual POS/accounting format. Add store-scoped customers, explicit opening debt, credit-sale obligations, payments with explicit allocation, reversal references and immutable audit history. No automatic debt inferred from a payment or purchase. Amounts remain integer paise. Test duplicate requests, partial allocation, overpayment policy, reversals, cross-store IDs, role permissions and offline replay. Add expense records with merchant-reviewed category and date. Identity/contact fields and retention must be designed before marketing.
2. **Staff tasks and local reminders.** Persist task definition, assignee membership, due time/timezone, completion and reopen history; expose overdue/open/completed views by role. Distinguish in-app reminders from delivered pushes. Tasks should not mutate stock, debt or recovery. Add a durable deduplication key and acknowledgement/snooze model before notifications.
3. **Manual receipts and cash scenarios.** Require opening cash/bank balances, declared source coverage and explicit upcoming obligations. Show scenarios with assumptions and unknowns, not an authoritative whole-business balance. Add customer reminder drafts from chosen allocations; require exact recipient/content review. Payment links need official merchant integration, not invented URLs.
4. **Supplier performance and stock exceptions.** Define expected delivery dates and distinguish partial deliveries, overdelivery, damage and missing counts. Show traceable counts/delays and missing coverage; avoid unsupported reliability scores. Use explicit merchant-reviewed adjustment thresholds for exception flags. Dead-stock detection needs a daily sales/zero-sales/stockout observation calendar; a silent ledger cannot establish demand.
5. **Customer marketing.** Add specific revocable contact/purpose/channel consent, opt-out, campaign drafts, reviewed recipient snapshots and expiry. Start with local draft/export only. Real sending requires authorised WABA/templates, explicit approval, provider message IDs and delivery receipts, bounded retries and ambiguous-send handling. Segments and dormancy require actual covered purchase history; no inferred sensitive attributes.
6. **Accounting preparation.** Add explicit GST/source fields and validate deterministic totals without inventing tax treatment. A qualified CA must review the export contract and exception handling. Expense categories are reviewed suggestions. Compliance reminders need merchant applicability and maintained official deadline sources. Local exports are not filed returns.
7. **Live payment ingestion.** Obtain official authorised Paytm contracts and merchant mapping. Implement authenticated ingestion, pagination/checkpoints, event versioning and provider identity, source freshness, replay ordering, and atomic receipts. Compare actual captured/settled/fee/refund components. Test with official fixtures and authorised sandbox before marking live reconciliation implemented.
8. **Forecast/procurement/growth.** Establish observed sales/depletion coverage, zero-sales versus missing intervals, unit conversions, supplier lead times and stockout handling. Backtest against a simple baseline by SKU/time holdout. Procurement additionally needs current permissioned offers, draft-order approval/cancellation/confirmation state machine. Do not turn off readiness gates to simulate implementation.
9. **Lending/benchmarking.** Require a named authorised lender, partner-owned checklist/decisions and specific sharing/withdrawal rules. No local model makes creditworthiness decisions. Benchmark releases require a consented comparable cohort and reviewed suppression/privacy policy. These cannot be completed from a general-purpose AI key.
10. **Release and evidence.** Each schema change needs forward migration and rollback/data-preservation planning, transaction/version/idempotency tests, dynamic anonymous/tenant coverage, offline conflict tests where eligible and mobile browser checks. Keep PostgreSQL server truth and IndexedDB draft truth separate. Rebuild/test native packaging after UI changes; test a physical device before claiming camera/mic/process-kill/background reliability.
